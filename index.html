About Us

I'm the driver myself, not a service.

1,00,000+
KM of driving experience
10+
States covered

Bharat Ride Tours India started with a simple thought — many people want to travel by two-wheeler, but either they don't know how to ride, or they're afraid of the highway, or they simply have no one to go with.

I have my own scooty, my own helmet, my own license, PUC and insurance — everything ready. I've personally ridden the routes of Rajasthan, UP, Uttarakhand, Delhi-NCR, and Himachal — from Agra, Gwalior, Jhansi to Nainital, Haridwar, Rishikesh, Purnagiri, Kainchi Dham.

Where a 4-wheeler group waits around and costs more, you can set off solo — without a worry.
