Bharat Ride Tours India ek simple soch se shuru hua — bahut log two-wheeler pe ghoomna chahte hain, lekin ya toh chalana nahi aata, ya highway se darr lagta hai, ya bas koi saath nahi hota.

Meri apni scooty hai, apna helmet, apna license, PUC aur insurance — sab kuch ready. Rajasthan, UP, Uttarakhand, Delhi-NCR aur Himachal ke rastey main khud ghoom chuka hoon — Agra, Gwalior, Jhansi se lekar Nainital, Haridwar, Rishikesh, Purnagiri, Kainchi Dham tak.

Jahan 4-wheeler group ka wait karta hai aur budget bhi zyada hota hai, wahan aap akele bhi nikal sakte hain — bina kisi chinta ke.
